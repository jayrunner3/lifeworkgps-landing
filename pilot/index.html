How long is pilot pricing available?

Through December 31, 2026, or until a limited plan is full, whichever comes first. New pricing takes effect January 1, 2027.

What does unlimited hiring mean on the Founder's and Pilot plans?

Unlimited hires in months 1 to 6. Months 7 to 12 include 25 hires a month. Past that, each additional hire is $500 on the Founder's Plan or $1,000 on the Pilot Plan. Both plans have a 6-month minimum term and run up to 12 months.

What happens when my pilot plan ends?

You choose a standard LifeWork plan. Founder's and Pilot customers keep 15% off list prices for as long as they remain customers.

Can I test it before committing?

Yes. Request a consult and a customer care teammate will walk you through options for trying LifeWork with your team first.

Do you support evergreen roles?

Yes. Evergreen roles are the ones you are always hiring for, where you keep a pipeline of candidates warm instead of paying per hire. They are set up with a customer success teammate, so reach out and we will walk you through it.
